Dashboard Award Details

About these numbers

Data Source: USAspending.gov bulk download API, filtered to CFDA 10.868 (REAP)

Key Fields:
• federal_action_obligation: New money committed (positive) or deobligated (negative)
• total_outlayed_amount_for_overall_award: Cumulative amount actually paid out (as of data date)
• period_of_performance_current_end_date: the end of the award's period of performance

Data as of:

What "outlays" means here: cumulative outlays to date, as of the date the file was downloaded, for each award. The field repeats the same cumulative value on every transaction row of an award, so every outlay figure on this page counts each award once. A blank value means no outlay was reported and counts as $0. USAspending notes that outlays have only been required to be reported since FY2022 (FY2020 for COVID-19-funded awards), so outlays on FY2021 awards may be incomplete.

Known limitation: because outlays are cumulative per award, the data cannot say when an outlay happened. There is no monthly or per-fiscal-year outlay series here. Wherever outlays appear by period, the period is the fiscal year in which the award was first obligated in this data, and the figure is that group's outlays to date. Obligations (new money, deobligations) are per transaction and are shown by the month of the action.

Outlay rate = outlays to date ÷ net obligations (obligations minus deobligations) in this FY2021-2025 data.

Counts:

State and recipient breakdowns (in the data file; not charted on this page): an award that lists more than one state or recipient name is counted in full in each, so these outlays add up to slightly more than the total:

Expired / active split (in the data file, not shown in the charts) and "years elapsed" use the date the data file was generated (), not the date of the data.

Monthly Obligations by Fiscal Year (positive obligations, by action month)

Outlays to Date vs. Net Obligations, by Fiscal Year of First Obligation

Groups awards by the fiscal year of their first positive obligation in this data. For each group: the share of the group's net obligations (all its obligations minus deobligations, in any year) that has been outlayed to date, and the share not yet outlayed. These are cumulative outlays as of the data date, not outlays made in that fiscal year. Recent groups have had less time to be paid out.

Monthly Deobligations by Fiscal Year (by action month)

Unspent Money by Award End Year (net obligations minus outlays to date; only end years 2024 and later are charted)

For awards first obligated in each fiscal year, unspent funds (net obligations in this data minus outlays to date, floored at $0 for each bar), grouped by Award End Year. Award End Year is the year of the period_of_performance_current_end_date on the award's latest transaction by action date (ties: the later end date, then the larger transaction key); that field is the end of the award's period of performance. Only end years 2024 and later are charted.
